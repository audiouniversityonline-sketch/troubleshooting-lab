Stem A/B Test

This page plays the raw .wav stems completely outside the Troubleshooting Lab app — no console, no EQ, no metering, no React. It exists to answer one question: does the file itself sound right on your machine?

Raw plays the buffer straight to your speakers at unity gain (nothing touches it). Mixer path plays the same buffer through the exact chain the app uses for a band stem in v57 — a 20 Hz high-pass (off, transparent) and a unity gain, and nothing else. If Raw and Mixer path sound identical, the app's signal path is transparent. If Raw already sounds wrong, it is your output or monitoring, not the app. Only one clip plays at a time so you can A/B cleanly.
